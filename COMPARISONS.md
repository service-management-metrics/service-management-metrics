# Comparisons

How this book relates to nearby works. Descriptions are of what each work is about, kept general on purpose. Check the originals for detail.

| Work | What it covers | How this book differs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Service Management Guide](https://github.com/service-management-guide/service-management-guide) | Practices for running IT services the agile way | This book is its metrics companion. It measures those practices and does not repeat them. Topics summarize and point to the guide. |
| [Agile ITIL Metrics](https://github.com/agile-itil-metrics/agile-itil-metrics) | The parent book on measuring agile IT service management | This book keeps its discipline and generalizes it: ITIL 5, enterprise service management, and Part 10 on AI, automation, and analytics. |
| [Software Engineering Metrics](https://github.com/software-engineering-metrics/software-engineering-metrics) | Measuring software engineering | This book adds service management, operations, experience, cost, and risk, and keeps the same discipline of pairing every metric with a gaming vector and a guardrail. |
| ITIL 4 | Guidance for IT service management, including measurement and reporting practices | ITIL describes practices. This book picks measures for them, with ways each can mislead. It summarizes ITIL and does not reproduce it. |
| DORA research and the Four Keys | Software delivery performance measures | Covered as one family among many (see topic 2.8, DORA metrics), alongside service desk, reliability, experience, and cost. |
| The SPACE framework | A multi-dimensional view of developer productivity | Used as input to the team and developer experience measures (see topic 9.1). |
| The Google SRE books | Reliability engineering, service level objectives, error budgets | Drawn on for reliability measures (see Part 5). This book also covers the non-engineering side of services. |

This book is not a certification guide, a vendor comparison, or a benchmark report.
