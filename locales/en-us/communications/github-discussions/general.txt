Say hello and tell us how you use Service Management Metrics

Welcome to the Service Management Metrics Discussions. This is the place for introductions, opinions, and open conversation about the book.

I am a maintainer of the project. I wrote Service Management Metrics because I wanted a practical handbook for measuring service management in the age of ITIL 5, through four lenses: agile, AI, automation, and analytics, with the gaming vector and guardrail for every metric to be easy to find and use. I would like to hear who is reading it and why.

**One question to start:** Which one service management metric does your team track today, and how could it be gamed?

A sentence or two is plenty. Replies to other people are welcome too.
