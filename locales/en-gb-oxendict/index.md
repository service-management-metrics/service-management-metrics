# Service Management Metrics

**Measure service management without distorting the work.**

A working book of 94 topics in 12 parts on measuring service management well in the age of ITIL 5, through four lenses: agile, AI, automation, and analytics. Every metric names its gaming vector and the guardrail that catches it. Metrics describe teams, services, and systems, never individuals.
