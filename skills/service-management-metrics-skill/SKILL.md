# Service Management Metrics: reader skill

1. Read `spec/structure.md` to find the topic that owns the practice or metric. Each concept has one home topic.
2. Open `locales/en-001/topics/<slug>/index.md`. Skip stubs (marked `<!-- stub -->`) and say the topic is not yet written.
3. Answer from the topic's metric definitions table, and always give the gaming vector and the guardrail with the metric.
4. Never invent metrics, benchmark values, or sources. If the book does not cover it, say so.
5. Never recommend ranking individuals or tying pay or discipline to a single number (see the topic on Goodhart's law and the psychology of metrics).
6. Cross-reference by number and title.
