# Request for comments

This book is a first complete draft. These are the questions the maintainer most wants help with.

## What I want to learn

1. **Which metrics are wrong, risky, or missing?** Especially any whose gaming vector or guardrail you have seen fail in practice.
2. **Which sources are wrong?** Books, papers, standards, and guidance documents were cited from memory and only partly verified (see `locales/en-001/topics/references-and-further-reading/index.md`).
3. **Does the agile stance read well?** The book emphasizes continuous delivery, continuous stakeholder involvement, continuous improvement, ongoing evolution, sustainable pace, and motivated individuals, and treats sprints, Scrum, and Kanban only conditionally. Is that clear and fair?
4. **Are the worked examples believable?** They use illustrative figures and no real companies. Do they match what you see?
5. **Are the translations natural?** For any locale you read natively.

## Useful kinds of feedback

A specific topic number and sentence, what is wrong, and what you would write instead. A source for any claim you dispute is best.

## How to send it

Open an issue on <https://github.com/service-management-metrics/service-management-metrics/issues>, or email <joel@joelparkerhenderson.com>.
